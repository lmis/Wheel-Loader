Backgrounds, pages 1 to 14

1. Copy and paste

Each prompt has the style and the scene in one. Make each picture 2560 × 1440; the three street and sky pages are wider strips, 5120 × 1440.

2. Only the place

The book adds the characters and everything that moves. Positions are a share of the picture’s width and height, from the top left.

3. Send them back

Name each file as shown on its card and attach it here. JPG is fine and keeps the book small.

Mama in parts

The same three steps as Papa and the twins. Attach Papa’s reference picture when you make Mama’s reference, so she comes out in the same style and shape, then attach Mama’s own reference for her two sheets. She has seven heads, because she has a worried face for the rainy station and a kissing face for goodnight.

Already in the book

Kept here in case a piece needs redoing. The book moves each character as a puppet: a body, a head, a tail, two legs and two arms, each turning on its own joint, with one head per expression.
