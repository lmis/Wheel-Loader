Mama Comes Home: painted backgrounds

One prompt per page of the book. Each picture is only the place: no characters and nothing that moves. The book keeps drawing and animating Mama, Papa, Léo, Louis, the robots and every trick on top of your painting.

1. Copy and paste

Each “Copy prompt” button copies the style line and the page’s scene together. Paste that into your image AI.

2. Make it 2560 × 1440

That’s the book’s 16:9 shape. The three street and sky pages are wider strips: 5120 × 1440. JPG is fine and keeps the book small.

3. Send them back

Name each file as shown on its card and attach it in the chat, or drop it into candybook/art/. I’ll put it in the book.

Try these three first

Morning (page 1), the office (page 4) and the rainy station (page 11) cover sunny, indoor and night. Once those look right in the book, do the rest with the same settings so all the pages match.

The style line

Already included in every page prompt below. Copy it on its own if your image AI has a separate style or “describe the look” box.

The fifteen pages

Positions are given as a share of the picture’s width and height, measured from the top left, so the painting lines up with where the book draws characters and furniture. Anything listed under “The book adds” must stay out of the painting.

Later: the characters

For the step after backgrounds. Image AI finds it hard to draw the same character twice, so start each character from one reference sheet and reuse that picture as the reference for every later pose. Ask for a plain white background.
